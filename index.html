Recommendations for training and evaluation data

  • Add paired examples of standard and non-standard spelling (risiko/resiko, silakan/silahkan, mengubah/merubah) with the standard form marked as preferred.
  • Include idioms and fixed expressions with natural Indonesian equivalents, so the model learns meaning instead of word-for-word translation.
  • Add dialogue data across registers (formal, neutral, casual, regional) and test whether the model matches the user’s tone and pronouns.
  • Evaluate summaries and translations on faithfulness separately from fluency, because fluent text can hide serious factual errors.
